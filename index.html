Why Donate Locally?

Donating usable items can keep them out of the trash while making everyday necessities more accessible to other community members. Iowa City is home to students, families, and longtime residents who can all benefit from a culture of sharing and reuse.

Even a small donation, such as a winter coat or a collection of children's books, can have a meaningful second life.

Quick Donation Checklist

Before dropping off your donations, follow these five simple steps:

  1. Sort your items: Separate clothing, books, furniture, and household goods.
  2. Check the condition: Make sure items are clean, safe, and in good working condition.
  3. Find a donation location: Choose an organization that accepts the items you want to donate.
  4. Confirm donation guidelines: Check accepted items, drop-off hours, and any restrictions.
  5. Pack and donate: Organize your items in bags or boxes and deliver them to your chosen location.

Important Donation Reminder

Donation policies, accepted items, and drop-off hours can change. Before visiting a donation location, contact the organization directly or check its official website to confirm current guidelines. This can help you avoid unnecessary trips and ensure your items are accepted.
