Where to Donate Furniture in Iowa City

Moving, redecorating, or replacing old furniture? Discover ways to give your gently used furniture a new home in the Iowa City area.

Furniture Donation Options in Iowa City

Donating furniture can help other community members furnish their homes while keeping reusable items out of landfills. Several types of local organizations may accept furniture, depending on its condition, size, and current needs.

Habitat for Humanity ReStore

The Iowa City ReStore is a local option for donating reusable furniture and household materials. Donated items are resold to support Iowa Valley Habitat for Humanity's housing programs.

Best for: Gently used tables, chairs, shelving, and other usable home furnishings.

ReStore also offers a donation pickup request service for items that are difficult to transport.

Review ReStore donation information

Community Furniture Assistance Programs

Some nonprofit organizations help individuals and families obtain essential furniture when moving into stable housing. These programs may need practical items such as beds, dining tables, and dressers.

Best for: Clean, functional furniture that can help someone furnish a home.

Contact an organization before donating to confirm which items are currently needed.

Local Thrift Stores

Some thrift stores in the Iowa City area accept furniture for resale. Their acceptance policies may depend on available storage space and the condition of the furniture.

Best for: Smaller furniture, end tables, chairs, and home accessories.

Call ahead before bringing large or heavy items.

Neighborhood Sharing Groups

Community sharing networks can connect people who have extra furniture with residents looking for affordable or free furnishings.

Best for: Desks, bookshelves, coffee tables, and other furniture that can be picked up directly.

This may be especially useful for students moving between apartments.

What Types of Furniture Can You Donate?

Donation organizations generally prefer furniture that is clean, sturdy, and ready to use. Common examples include:

  • Dining tables and chairs
  • Desks and office chairs
  • Bookshelves and storage units
  • Dressers and nightstands
  • Coffee tables and end tables
  • Sofas and armchairs in good condition
  • Bed frames and other accepted bedroom furniture

Not every organization accepts all of these items. Mattresses, upholstered furniture, and especially large pieces may have additional restrictions.

How to Prepare Furniture for Donation

  1. Check the condition. Make sure the furniture is stable, functional, and free of major damage.
  2. Clean the furniture. Remove dust, stains, pet hair, and other debris.
  3. Check for missing parts. Confirm that drawers, shelves, screws, and other essential pieces are included.
  4. Measure larger items. Record dimensions to help the receiving organization plan for transportation and storage.
  5. Confirm donation requirements. Contact the organization before arranging a drop-off or pickup.

Moving Out of a University of Iowa Apartment?

Moving season can leave students with furniture they no longer need. Rather than throwing away usable desks, chairs, or shelves, consider arranging a donation or sharing them with another student. Start planning early so you have time to coordinate transportation before your lease ends.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I donate furniture with scratches?

Minor cosmetic wear may be acceptable, but organizations often reject furniture with structural damage, broken parts, or significant stains. Check with the recipient first.

Can someone pick up my furniture donation?

Some organizations offer pickup services, especially for larger furniture. Availability may depend on location, staffing, and the items being donated.

Can I donate a used mattress?

Mattress acceptance policies vary because of cleanliness and safety concerns. Contact the organization directly before attempting to donate one.

What if my furniture cannot be donated?

If furniture is unsafe or too damaged to reuse, check local recycling and disposal options. Do not leave unwanted furniture outside a donation center without permission.
